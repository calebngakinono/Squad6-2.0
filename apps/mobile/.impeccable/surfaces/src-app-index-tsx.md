# Surface brief: Squad Six mobile app (all screens)

Scope: the whole iPhone app's visual world (replaces "clean premium"). Mode: Operate. Audience: adults restarting the habit, with their squad of up to six. Task: see where I and my squad stand this week, start a workout in one tap. Constraint: Caleb's pinned palette #ADB499 #435341 #2A3B2D #071E15 #284936 #26604A; native iOS controls; light and dark both supported.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The squad is a trail party. Every day trained is a painted blaze on your tree, and nobody walks the trail alone. Refuses the category default of white cards, rings and one accent.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest ground (#071E15) with forest and olive surfaces (#284936, #2A3B2D, #435341), sage (#ADB499) as the one action and highlight colour, emerald (#26604A) for selection in light mode. Condensed routed-sign display face (Barlow Condensed) for titles and numbers; Overpass (highway-sign lineage) for text. Small radii (4 to 8), hairline olive borders, rectangular painted blazes as the only mark.

STORY: Open the app, see your trail and your five teammates' trails side by side; know at a glance who has been out this week; start today's workout with one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Squad name in condensed caps top-left, Settings top-right. The trail sign: "This week" caption, my goal line, then six columns of blazes on one baseline (filled = day trained, hollow = goal day left, yours brightest, open seats dashed). Destinations as a sign board of rows below. Footer: "Run again" (sage, primary) beside "Other workout".

FORM: Trail Blaze, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned by the roll). seed:0aeed3ead38dfe8c. Raises: fixed standings columns (split-flap), filled/hollow day cells (timetable), your trail brightest (transit), workout focus recedes the rest (streaming), all six at one scale on one baseline (botanical folio).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
